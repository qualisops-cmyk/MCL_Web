About MCL

Insight. Decision. Delivery.

The institutions that respond well to change will be the ones that learn faster than the change around them. Institutions face concrete pressures regarding research performance, accreditation, international standing, links with industry, and graduates' readiness for technology-intensive work.

Mekong Capability Lab works at the interface of universities, employers, investors, and government. We build capability—what an institution, firm, or partnership can reliably do after the advisers leave—through a lab approach that treats change as a series of tested iterations.

1. Diagnose

We establish where things stand, using tested instruments and data to form an empirical baseline.

2. Co-design

We develop strategies, curricula, and partnerships alongside the people who will actually own them.

3. Test

We pilot changes at a controlled scale where they can be closely observed and corrected.

4. Measure & Adapt

We track results rigorously and refine the methodology until the institutional change holds.

Core Domains

Our Services

We organize our work into five core areas, each supported by rigorous diagnostic tools and empirical evidence standards.

Institutional Strategy, Quality, and Accreditation

We guide institutions through higher education strategic planning, governance, autonomy, and accountability. We advise on international accreditation pathways and develop quality assurance systems.

Diagnostics and Due Diligence

We conduct institutional performance audits, AI-readiness diagnostics, and academic integrity audits. We provide due diligence for investors and program evaluation for development partners.

Innovation and Partnerships

We provide innovation ecosystem assessment, analyze research value chains, and design academic-industry partnerships. We guide universities through multi-year transformation partnerships.

Talent and Skills Strategy

We deliver labor-market outlooks to guide investment and policy. We develop sector talent plans for priority industries (like semiconductors) and workforce strategies for companies.

Teaching and Learning for the AI Era

We assist institutions with curriculum and pedagogy reform for resilience to AI, personalized learning strategies, faculty and instructor development, and maintaining academic integrity in a rapidly shifting technological environment.

Leadership

The Team

MCL's principals bring an inside view of Vietnamese higher education combined with deep experience in global labor markets and innovation systems.

Dr. Ninh Nguyen

Executive Director

Dr. Nguyen connects Vietnam's universities with international practice, including international quality assurance and accreditation. She led a comparative study on university autonomy that directly informed the amendment of Vietnam's Higher Education Law.

She developed performance indicators for Vietnam's public universities with World Bank support. She co-designed USAID's Partnership for Higher Education Reform and served as its Deputy Chief of Party.

She also managed VinUniversity's Transformation Management Office to drive its push toward research-intensive performance, and she led the mid-term evaluation of USAID's grants to Fulbright University Vietnam.

Phil Psilos

Deputy Director & Head of Consulting

Phil helps governments, universities, companies, and investors build skills and innovation capacity, drawing on three decades of experience across the United States, Europe, Asia, and Africa.

A former Deloitte consultant and advisor to the U.S. Governors, he served as Chief of Party of USAID's higher education reform programs in Vietnam, where he advised the Ministry of Education and Training and the leadership of Vietnam's national universities. He designed the USAID STRIDE program in the Philippines and its Innovation Ecosystem Assessment.

He also founded TransitionScience.ai to model AI transition frictions, applying this to future-proof core curricula and develop workforce strategies for the AI era.
